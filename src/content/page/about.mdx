      **EnviroPlus Consulting, LLC (EPC)** has provided an ever-increasing
      diversity of biological, archaeological, and paleontological consulting
      and related services to the government and private sectors since 1987.
      Some of these services include:


      * Preparation of biological assessments, habitat assessments, and similar
      documents in support of permitting.

      * Special status species surveys for invertebrates, fish, amphibians,
      reptiles, birds and raptors, and small and large mammals, and plants
      according to established survey protocols.

      * Biological monitoring in support of construction projects. Typical
      project types include transmission lines, pipelines, road and highway
      projects, solar and wind energy development, and commercial construction.

      * Phase I archaeological surveys and records searches, Native American
      consultation, and paleontological records searches.

      * Specialized biological research and study design.

      * Ground and airborne radio tracking, helicopter surveys and aerial
      photography.

      * Ground-up development of biological/environmental training programs and
      videos.


      EPC has distinguished itself by maintaining outstanding relations with
      both regulatory agencies and industry. Regulatory agencies know us for the
      high quality of our field and report work, adherence to permit
      requirements, and transparent relations. Industry clients recognize our
      ability to understand and find solutions to problems, timely delivery of
      product, and the ability to work with field staff from diverse industries.
